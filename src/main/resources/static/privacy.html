Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register a customer or seller account on VELMART, place an order, or enter shipping details, we collect information including your name, email address, contact phone number, and delivery addresses. We securely hash all passwords using BCrypt encryption before storing them in our Amazon DynamoDB database. Plaintext passwords are never visible or logged.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used exclusively to maintain your persistent shopping bag, fulfill Cash on Delivery orders, deliver packages to your designated address, facilitate order tracking, and prevent fraudulent abuse.

3. Cookies & Session Storage

VELMART uses secure, HttpOnly session cookies (VELMART_JSESSIONID) with SameSite protection to maintain your active authentication session. Sensitive credentials are never stored in browser localStorage.

4. Data Security

All communication between client browsers, Nginx reverse proxy, Spring Boot Java services, and AWS DynamoDB occurs over encrypted HTTPS connections using TLS. Administrative operations are tracked in encrypted audit logs.
