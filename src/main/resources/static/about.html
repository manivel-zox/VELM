About VELMART

VELMART is a full-stack, enterprise-grade e-commerce shopping platform built to bring trust, premium curated catalogs, and effortless shopping to customers across India. Powered by a modern Java 21, Spring Boot 3.x, and Amazon DynamoDB architecture, VELMART delivers ultra-fast page speeds, secure session authentication, and transactional concurrency for inventory and orders.

Our Mission

To provide a reliable marketplace connecting verified manufacturers and sellers of premium watches, electronics, fashion, and home lifestyle products with discerning shoppers, offering transparent pricing in Indian Rupees (₹), convenient Cash on Delivery, and reliable courier delivery.

Why Choose VELMART?

  • Genuine Brand Authenticity: Every product listed by our seller partners undergoes quality checks and specifications verification.
  • Server-Side Stock Verification: Concurrency-safe shopping cart and transactional checkout ensure you never experience unfulfilled orders.
  • Cash on Delivery Available: Inspect your watch or fashion product upon doorstep arrival before paying.
  • Easy Pre-Shipment Cancellation: Transparent customer policies allow instant order cancellations before items are dispatched.

College Capstone Project

VELMART is engineered as a high-performance capstone software engineering project demonstrating modern cloud-native principles, secure role-based access control (RBAC), DynamoDB NoSQL indexing, and scalable AWS Lightsail deployment.
